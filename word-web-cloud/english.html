分阶段词汇重点

同一个单词要放回句子中理解。下面选取四个词库中的代表词，展示词义与完整例句；上方词表可继续查看全部词汇、发音和掌握状态。

小学词汇

先掌握日常场景中的基础表达。

  • about关于；大约We talk about the story.
  • above在……上面The picture is above the bed.
  • active活跃的He is an active boy.
  • activity活动My favourite activity is swimming.
  • after在……之后We play after school.
  • afternoon下午Good afternoon, teacher.

初中词汇

结合词性与句子结构理解常见用法。

  • ability能力Hard work and ability matter.
  • able能够的；有能力的She is able to swim.
  • abolish废除；取消Slavery was abolished.
  • about关于；大约What about you?
  • above在……上面The bird is above the tree.
  • abroad在国外；到国外My brother studies abroad.

高中词汇

关注熟词生义、搭配和抽象表达。

  • require需要；要求The project requires careful planning.
  • quality质量；品质This restaurant is known for the quality of its food.
  • concern担忧；关心Climate change is a global concern.
  • tend倾向于；照料Plants tend to grow faster in warm weather.
  • approach接近；处理We should approach the issue with an open mind.
  • promote促进；提升The government promotes renewable energy.

雅思词汇

在较完整的学术和社会语境中辨析词义。

  • abate减弱；降低This action would not have been sufficient to abate the odor nuisance.
  • abdomen腹部；腹腔The doctor examined the patient's abdomen for signs of pain.
  • abduction诱拐It is not clear what the kidnapper's motive was for carrying out the abduction.
  • abiding永久的；持久的Some member countries have been lax in abiding by G-7 agreements.
  • ability能力It is no wonder that a man of his ability is so successful.
  • aboriginal土著的The museum works with Aboriginal communities to preserve local history.
